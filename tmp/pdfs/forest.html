What is Forest?
Forest is a focus app that helps people stay off distracting apps by turning focus time into a growing virtual forest.

During each focus session, a tree grows while you stay focused. Open another app, and the tree dies. Over time, your sessions build into a visible record of how you spend your time.

Forest also includes app blocking, focus statistics, group focus with friends, digital wellbeing tools, and real tree planting through Trees for the Future.

As The New York Times put it: "Forest works well, and if your goal is to be more in the moment, ignore your phone and actually talk to your friends when you are with them, this is the app for you."

How does Forest help people stay focused?

Every focus session in Forest grows a virtual tree. If you leave the app or use blocked apps during strict sessions, the tree may wither.

That small sense of loss is intentional. Forest uses visible progress and gentle accountability to make distractions feel more noticeable and focus easier to return to over time.

Instead of only counting minutes, Forest turns focus into something you can see and keep.

Can Forest block Instagram, TikTok, and other apps?

Yes. Forest can block Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, YouTube, and other distracting apps you choose.

Deep Focus blocks selected apps during an active focus session. Time Guard lets you schedule app blocks throughout the day, outside of focus sessions as well. Both tools are free to use.

What makes Forest different from other focus apps?

Forest combines a focus timer with visible progress, app blocking, and real world impact.

A few things set it apart: app blocking is free on every tier, where most competitors charge for it. Plant Together lets a group share one focus session, where the whole group's tree dies if anyone gives up.

And Forest has helped fund over 2 million real trees through Trees for the Future since 2014. The overall approach is built around motivation, not pressure.

Does Forest actually plant real trees?

Yes. Forest partners with Trees for the Future, a nonprofit, to support real tree planting across Kenya, Senegal, Tanzania, Uganda, and Cameroon.

Users can spend earned coins to plant trees, turning focus time into real world impact. Since launching the program, Forest users have helped plant more than 2 million real trees. View Forest's sponsorship at Trees for the Future →

Does Forest help with ADHD?

People with ADHD often describe challenges with getting started, staying engaged, or feeling motivated by tasks with delayed rewards.

Forest is not a medical treatment for ADHD, but as a behavioral tool it speaks directly to these challenges. Visible progress, immediate feedback, and shorter structured focus sessions make effort feel more concrete. Growing trees and seeing your focus history over time can help turn abstract goals into something easier to notice and maintain.

Many users with ADHD say Forest feels easier to stick with because the rewards are visible, the feedback is immediate, and progress builds over time.

About Forest

What is Forest?
Forest is a focus app that helps people stay off distracting apps by turning focus time into a growing virtual forest. During each focus session, a tree grows while you stay focused. Open another app, and the tree dies. Over time, your sessions build into a visible record of how you spend your time.

Forest also includes app blocking, focus statistics, group focus with friends, digital wellbeing tools, and real tree planting through Trees for the Future.

As The New York Times put it: "Forest works well, and if your goal is to be more in the moment, ignore your phone and actually talk to your friends when you are with them, this is the app for you."

Since 2014, over 60 million people have used Forest.

What makes Forest different from other focus or pomodoro apps?

Most focus apps help you save time. Forest helps you spend it well. The heart of Forest is a tree: it grows while you focus, dies if you wander off. Your concentration becomes something visible — a record of time you chose. Around that, Forest packs app blocking (free on every tier — most competitors paywall theirs), screen time scheduling, mindfulness breaks, and synchronized group focus where the whole forest falls if anyone gives up. Forest also plants real trees on Earth — over 2 million and counting.

Forest is a companion, not a coach — no shame, no preaching, no productivity-bro language. Trusted by 60 million since 2014.

Can Forest help with ADHD and focus problems?

People with ADHD often describe challenges with getting started, staying engaged, or feeling motivated by tasks with delayed rewards.

Forest is not a medical treatment for ADHD, but as a behavioral tool it speaks directly to these challenges. Visible progress, immediate feedback, and shorter structured sessions make effort feel more concrete.

Many users with ADHD say Forest is easier to stick with because the rewards are visible, the feedback is immediate, and progress builds over time.

Does Forest help with phone addiction or screen time?

Forest is a behavioral tool, not a clinical treatment. Research by Ward et al. (2017) found that the mere physical presence of a smartphone reduces available cognitive capacity, even when users successfully resist checking it. Forest addresses this through a few mechanisms: the growing tree creates emotional stake in staying off your phone, app blocking physically stops other apps during sessions, and screen time scheduling sets limits throughout your day. Unlike passive screen time tools that only report usage, Forest gives you a concrete reason to stay focused. For clinical phone addiction, please consult a qualified professional.

What's the difference between Forest and iPhone Screen Time?

iPhone Screen Time reports usage and applies limits you configure ahead of time. Forest is an active commitment tool: you plant a tree at the start of a session, and the tree dies if you break it. Screen Time works at the macro level, such as daily app limits. Forest works at the moment level, when you need to commit right now. Most people find them complementary rather than competing.

Does Forest help with procrastination?

Yes. Procrastination often happens when tasks feel too large, rewards feel too far away, or getting started feels harder than continuing.

Forest makes focus feel more immediate. Growing trees, visible progress, and shorter structured sessions help turn abstract goals into something easier to begin.

Many users say Forest feels helpful because it creates a small reason to start now, instead of waiting to feel motivated.

Focus & Timer

What happens if I use other apps during a focus session?

With Deep Focus mode on, Forest blocks non-allowlisted apps and guides you back. To actually open another app, you'd have to tap "Give Up" — which kills your tree. Without Deep Focus, you can switch apps without consequence, but we recommend Deep Focus on for the stricter experience. Dead trees stay in your forest as an honest record of the journey, not a verdict. Forest doesn't hide failed sessions or shame you for them — what matters is the forest you grow over time.

Can I pause a focus session?

Yes (Pro/Plus only). You can pause when you need to (each pause lasts up to 5 minutes), and your tree is safe during a pause. If you find pause distracting, you can disable it in settings to keep your sessions strict end-to-end. Free users don't have access to pause.

What is Deep Focus mode?
Deep Focus is a stricter mode that blocks you from other apps or redirects you back to Forest when you try to open one. You can't toggle it off mid-session (the commitment is part of the point). Free on all tiers; Pro/Plus add a customizable App Allowlist for apps you're allowed to use.

Can Forest block Instagram, TikTok, and other social apps?

Yes. Forest can block Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, YouTube, or any other app you choose — through two complementary tools. Deep Focus blocks apps during a focus session: try to switch and Forest pulls you back, until you give up and let the tree die. Time Guard schedules recurring blocks throughout your day — no social media 9 to 5, no feeds before bed. Both are free; Plus extends them with custom App Allowlists and multi-app scheduling.

How do I use Plant Together (Focus Room)?

Create a room, invite friends via code, and everyone plants the same tree during a synchronized session. iOS and Android users can join the same room. Everyone shares the stake — if one person gives up, everyone's tree dies. It's cooperative accountability: you show up for each other, not just yourself.

Rest & Wellness

What is Mindful Space?
A sound-led breathing room for intentional rest with guided breathing. Choose from different music styles, ambient soundscapes, and immersive visual scenes — all crafted for deep restoration. Use it between focus sessions to recharge before the next one, or open it anytime you need a moment to slow down. Free includes one soundscape; Plus unlocks the full library.

Can I listen to music during focus?

Yes — Forest has a library of Ambient Sounds (rain, cafe, meadow, etc.) you can layer over your focus session. Individual tracks are purchasable with in-app coins. Plus gets rotating seasonal access.

Habits & Analytics

What is Focus Challenge?
Daily and monthly missions designed to help you build a consistent focus habit. Small daily missions reward you with coins and items; monthly arcs build toward a signature tree — making consistency feel rewarding, not forced.

What are Focus Tags?
Labels you assign to sessions — Study, Work, Writing, or custom tags (Pro/Plus). You can filter analytics by tag to see where your focus hours go.

What does Forest show me about my focus?

The most distinctive view is your visible forest — every focused session as a tree, your time made visible at a glance. Forest also shows total focus time, focus patterns by day, week, or month, streaks, and breakdowns by tags (Study, Work, Writing) and tree species. Together, these help you understand your own focus rhythm and where your hours really go.

Real Trees

How many real trees has Forest planted?

Forest has funded over 2 million real trees through Trees for the Future since 2014, with the number growing continuously — see the live counter on our homepage for the current total. Trees are planted across five sub-Saharan African countries (Kenya, Senegal, Tanzania, Uganda, Cameroon) as part of the Forest Garden Program, which trains local farmers in sustainable agroforestry. Forest covers the planting cost from app revenue.

Does Forest really plant real trees?

Yes. Forest partners with Trees for the Future, a nonprofit, to plant real trees across five countries in Africa. Over 2 million real trees have been planted through this partnership. Pro and Plus users can spend their earned coins to trigger a real tree being planted on their behalf — Forest covers the cost from app revenue.

Which organization plants the trees?

Trees for the Future (TFtF) is a US-based nonprofit that plants trees as part of reforestation and sustainable agriculture programs. They train local farmers to plant diverse tree species that restore soil, provide food, and generate long-term income.

How many real trees can I plant?

Pro and Plus users can spend accumulated coins earned from focus sessions to plant real trees — up to 5 real trees per account. Forest covers the planting cost from app revenue; Trees for the Future handles the actual planting.

How can I verify the trees are really planted?

Your planting record in the Forest app shows every real tree you've triggered. Trees for the Future publicly documents Forest's sponsorship on their official site, and publishes regional impact reports separately. Individual per-tree GPS verification isn't available — TFtF prioritizes planting scale and farmer training over per-tree telemetry.
